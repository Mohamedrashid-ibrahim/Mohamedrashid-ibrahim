## 👋 About Me

I am a finance graduate with data science training, focused on data analytics, financial reporting, and business intelligence.

My foundation in finance, covering reporting, budgeting, forecasting, and performance analysis, sits alongside hands-on skills in Python, SQL, and Excel. This lets me work on both sides of a problem: understanding what the numbers mean for the business, and building the analysis and dashboards that show it.

I am building toward roles in **data analytics, financial analysis, FinTech, and business intelligence**.
